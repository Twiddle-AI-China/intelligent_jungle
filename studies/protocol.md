# Phase 2–5 listening and playability protocol

## Purpose

This protocol separates three claims that are easy to conflate: decoder continuity, rule audibility, and instrument learnability. Passing one does not imply the others.

## Study A — perceptual direction and continuity

- Render matched 8-second pairs from the same safe anchor and seed.
- Change exactly one perceptual direction: brightness, roughness, harmonicity, transientness, density, or register.
- Randomize A/B order and include 20% null pairs.
- Ask for direction (A/B/no difference), confidence (1–7), discontinuity count, and identity preservation (1–7).
- Minimum gate: at least 75% direction accuracy after null correction, median identity preservation at least 5, and no catastrophic artifact in the accepted path.

## Study B — isolated rule audibility

Use identical seeds and user event logs. Compare rule-off with one rule-on:

- Cohesion / 聚合：“哪一版建立了共同节拍与调性，同时没有变成齐奏？”
- Alignment / 对齐：“哪一版传播了共同变化趋势，同时保留了不同身份？”
- Separation / 分离：“哪一版让声部更清楚，同时没有变成随机散开？”

Record forced choice, confidence, free description, and whether the participant heard an unwanted collapse, clone, or jump. The test operator must not reveal the condition.

## Study C — learnability and agency

Five participants, 15–20 minutes each. Give no explanation of internal parameters. Ask them to:

1. make the world more coherent;
2. make parts more distinct;
3. create a disturbance and let the world recover;
4. reproduce a previously marked state;
5. perform two contrasting 30-second takes.

Capture the session seed and event log, time to first correct action, task completion, normalized replay distance, active-control ratio, causal clarity (1–7), agency (1–7), surprise-with-control (1–7), and verbal strategy.

## Decision rules

- A failed decoder-continuity gate sends work back to the model/atlas layer, not to UI polishing.
- A rule that is measurable but not audible must be remapped or removed.
- A behavior that is audible but cannot be deliberately reproduced is an effect, not yet an instrument technique.
- Hardware and cosmological visual expansion remain out of scope until at least four of five participants complete tasks 1–4 and median agency is at least 5/7.
